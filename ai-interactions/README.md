# AI interaction records

The AI-assisted pair must preserve important actual conversations with GenAI.
Records should include the prompt and response, the suggestion made, the
students' decision (accepted, rejected, or modified), and verification and
limitations. Keep records attributable to the relevant work and do not fabricate
conversation histories.

Use this directory for approved interaction records. Do not place the
human-only pair's private research ideas, code, visualizations, or conclusions
here.
